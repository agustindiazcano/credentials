## Graduate Studies

### MSc in Information Systems Engineering
**Universidad Tecnológica Nacional (UTN), Facultad Regional Buenos Aires - 2024 to Present**

🔗 [Official program page](https://frba.utn.edu.ar/posgrado/maestria-en-ingenieria-en-sistemas-de-informacion/)

Postgraduate program accredited **Category A** by CONEAU (Argentina's national university accreditation commission — the highest rating awarded). 700 total hours across foundational, elective, and research components.

- 69% of faculty (11 of 16 professors) hold a Doctoral degree, including the program's Director and Co-Director, both PhDs in Computer Science (Universidad Nacional de La Plata).
- Active research lines in Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, and Blockchain.
- Coursework: Intelligent Information Systems, Project Management and Formulation, Intelligent Data Exploitation, Business Intelligence (Data Warehouse & Data Mining), Smart Contract Design for Blockchain, Conceptual Modeling of Information Systems, Computer Forensics.

Key Artificial Intelligence topics covered within this coursework include genetic algorithms, artificial neural networks, and fuzzy logic in engineering applied within the Intelligent Information Systems and Intelligent Data Exploitation courses.
